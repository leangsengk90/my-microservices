What it does

The front door of the platform

One place to verify users, so the other services don't each have to handle passwords.

🔑

Sign-in

Email/password and social sign-in, with rate limiting on failed attempts.

🎟️

JWT tokens

Short-lived signed access tokens that other services can verify on their own.

🛡️

Roles & permissions

Role-based access rules that decide who can do what.

♻️

Session refresh

Refresh tokens keep users signed in without asking for the password again.
